How this book works

This is a textbook about artificial intelligence that goes unusually deep on one AI system: Claude. You'll learn how AI works, who builds it, how to use it well, and how to set it up, test it and teach it to others. Every chapter is written in plain words first. Technical detail comes later, and it's optional.

Who it's for

Anyone who uses AI and wants to really understand it. You don't need to be a developer. If you can use email and a web browser, you can start at Chapter 1. Readers who already know the basics can jump straight to the part they need: the level label on every chapter tells you where you are.

Non-technical readers

Testers, analysts, managers, designers. You'll understand how AI works and become a confident, skilful user of Claude.

Newer developers

One or two years of experience. You'll fill the gaps (how models work, the terminal, APIs) and learn to build with AI.

Team leads and trainers

You'll be able to explain AI to others, set Claude up for a whole team, and teach it with the exercises in each chapter.

Four promises

  1. Plain words first. Every idea is explained in everyday language before any technical term appears. When a technical word is needed, it's explained the first time it's used, and you can tap any dotted-underlined word to see its meaning.
  2. Depth when you want it. Sections marked Under the hood explain how things work inside. They're optional: skip them on a first read and nothing later will break.
  3. Practice, not just reading. Every chapter has a hands-on exercise, a quiz and an assignment.
  4. Checked facts. Anything that changes (model names, prices, commands, release dates) is checked against official sources and marked with the date it was last verified.

How to study

  1. Read one chapter at a time. Each shows reading and practice time at the top.
  2. Do the "Try it" exercise. Reading about AI without using it is like reading about swimming.
  3. Take the quiz. Wrong answers are useful: they show what to reread.
  4. Explain it. Use the "Teach it" box. If you can explain it out loud without notes, you've learned it.
  5. Bookmark what you'll come back to. Use ☆ Bookmark at the top of any chapter; bookmarked chapters show a ★ in the contents. Bookmarks are kept in your own browser only.
Use Claude to learn Claude

Keep Claude open while you study. Ask it to explain a paragraph differently, quiz you, or give another example. Treat its answers the way a good tester treats any result: check anything surprising.

How accuracy is handled

AI changes every month. A book like this can easily look impressive while containing invented details, so it follows strict rules:

  • Facts that change over time (models, prices, features, commands) are checked against official documentation, and the chapter shows a Verified 9 Nov 2026 date.
  • Commands for Claude Code were checked against the installed version's own help output (version 2.1.319) as well as Anthropic's documentation.
  • Predictions and opinions are placed in clearly labelled Opinion boxes, separate from facts.
  • If something couldn't be confirmed, the text says so rather than guessing.
  • Features can differ by plan, region and company settings. Where that matters, the chapter says so.
